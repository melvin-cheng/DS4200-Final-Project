Figure 3: Top Pizzas

Figure 3 shows the top five pizzas based on the selected metric. When the chart is sorted by revenue, the Thai Chicken Pizza ranks first at about $45,000, followed by the Barbecue Chicken Pizza at around $43,000. The Classic Deluxe Pizza brings in around $38,000, the Hawaiian Pizza earns about $33,000, and the Pepperoni Pizza ranks fifth with almost $30,000. This shows that chicken-based pizzas continue to dominate sales, but classic flavor pizzas also contribute to revenue.

When the chart is sorted by popularity, the ranking may change because some pizzas sell more frequently even if their total revenue is lower. This means a pizza can appear lower on the revenue list but higher in popularity if it has a lower price but a high number of orders. The most popular pizzas in order are the Thai Chicken Pizza, the Barbecue Chicken Pizza, the Classic Deluxe Pizza, the Hawaiian Pizza, and the Pepperoni Pizza.

Overall, the results highlight that revenue and popularity do not always match, and switching between the two views helps reveal whether a pizza is valuable because it sells at a high price or because it sells often. Try switching between revenue and popularity to compare the two patterns, and try hovering over the bars to learn more about the total price and order count.
