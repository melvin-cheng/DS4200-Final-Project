Size / Revenue Contribution (Stacked)

This stacked bar chart compares how each pizza size contributes to total revenue within each category. Across all categories (Chicken, Classic, Supreme, and Veggie), the large (L) and medium (M) sizes make up the biggest share of revenue, shown by the wide yellow and orange sections. Small (S) pizzas consistently contribute the least, and XL and XXL sizes rarely appear. The Classic category has the highest total height, meaning it generates the most revenue overall. Overall, the chart shows that larger sizes dominate revenue regardless of pizza type.
