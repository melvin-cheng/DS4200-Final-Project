Heatmap — Orders (Weekly / Daily)

This heatmap shows how order volume changes across the week and throughout each day. The darkest red blocks demonstrate that the highest order activity consistently occurs around 12 PM to 1 PM on weekdays, with Thursday and Friday showing the strongest midday peaks. A second noticeable cluster appears in the early evening between 5 PM and 7 PM, especially mid-week. Weekends have lighter colors overall, meaning lower and more evenly distributed order counts. Overall, the chart shows predictable lunchtime spikes during weekdays and mild evening activity later in the day.
