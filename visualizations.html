Unit price visualization

Interactive Altair visualization (same content as the single-page export).

Altair — This Altair figure links pizza prices with ingredient demand to help explore patterns in the menu. The scatter plot on the left shows the unit price of every pizza by category. This shows how Classic pizzas cluster tightly around mid-range prices, and Chicken and Supreme pizzas show more differences. When the x-axis is dragged to highlight a specific price range, the bar chart on the right updates to show the top five ingredients used in the pizzas within that selected price window, along with the total quantities sold. This interaction makes it easy to see which ingredients appear most often in higher-priced pizzas versus lower-priced ones, and how ingredient popularity shifts across categories and price points. Overall, the graph helps connect pricing, category differences, and topping demand in one combined view.
