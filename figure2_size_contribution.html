Figure 2 — Size Contribution to Total Sales by Category

Figure 2 Analysis

Figure 2 shows how different pizza sizes contribute to total revenue within each category. Classic pizzas have the highest total revenue at about $220,000. Supreme pizzas follow with around $208,000, Veggie pizzas reach about $195,000, and Chicken pizzas total roughly $198,000. Within every category, medium and large pizzas contribute most to the revenue. In Classic pizzas, medium and large sizes together contribute close to $160,000 of the total. Supreme pizzas are similar, with these two sizes adding up to around $150,000. Chicken and Veggie pizzas also show strong revenue from medium and large sizes, each reaching well over $120,000. Small pizzas add a smaller amount, usually between $30,000 and $50,000, depending on the category. XL and XXL sizes contribute very little, often below $20,000, and in some categories, closer to $10,000. Overall, the numbers show that most revenue comes from the middle sizes, while very small and very large sizes contribute only a small share. This means that customers focus their purchases on medium and large pizzas, making these sizes the main drivers of sales across every category.
